Questions

Does KLANG upload my samples anywhere?

No. Analysis, search and voice transcription all run on your computer. Your audio files are never uploaded.

Do I need to be online?

Only once, the first time you launch KLANG, to download its AI models (about 430 MB). After that it works fully offline.

Is this a subscription?

No. Free is free forever, and Pro is a one-time payment that includes every 1.x update.

What happens when I reach 2,500 samples on Free?

Everything you've already indexed keeps working. To index more sounds or add more folders, upgrade to Pro.

Which file types does it support?

Your library: WAV, AIFF and FLAC samples, plus Vital, Surge XT and u-he presets. Reference files for "find similar": WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3 and OGG.

Which DAWs does it work with?

Any DAW that accepts audio files dragged in from a folder, including Ableton Live, FL Studio, Reaper, Cubase, Studio One and Bitwig.

How does it understand what a sound is like?

KLANG uses an AI model trained on pairs of sounds and text descriptions, so words and audio can be compared directly. It also measures each sound's length, brightness, attack and loudness, which power the filters.

Is there a Mac version?

Yes. KLANG runs on Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Intel Macs aren't supported.
