## 6. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS AND COMPATIBILITY

KLANG is designed for high-performance audio processing with zero cloud reliance. Users must verify hardware compatibility before purchasing:

* **Windows:** Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit required; x86_64 architecture).
* **macOS:** macOS 14 Sonoma or later, executing natively on Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4 or newer). Intel-based Mac architectures are not supported.
* **Hardware:** Minimum 4 GB RAM (8 GB recommended), 500 MB free disk space for engine installation, plus local storage for user audio sample libraries.
* **DAW Compatibility:** Fully compatible with all Digital Audio Workstations supporting external file drag-and-drop (Ableton Live, FL Studio, Reaper, Cubase, Studio One, Bitwig, Logic Pro).
