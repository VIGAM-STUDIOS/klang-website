## 2. THE LOCAL-FIRST DESKTOP ARCHITECTURE (ZERO SOUND UPLOADS)

The KLANG desktop application executes all audio analysis, feature extraction, neural embeddings, and vector queries **100% locally on your personal computer**:

1. **Zero Audio Uploads:** Your sample libraries, sound recordings, instruments, stems, loops, and synthesizer presets are processed entirely on your local CPU and GPU. Your audio files are never uploaded, streamed, or transmitted to any server.
2. **Zero Audio Surveillance:** Directory structures, file names, audio durations, musical keys, waveforms, and sound tags remain strictly confined to your local hard drive.
3. **Local Vector Database:** The CLAP multimodal neural embeddings and HNSW vector index databases reside locally in your user application data folder and are never synchronized or backed up to VIGAM Labs infrastructure.
4. **Zero Desktop Telemetry:** The desktop application contains **no background analytics SDKs, no behavioral trackers, no telemetry probes, no automatic update pingers, and no automated crash reporting modules**.
